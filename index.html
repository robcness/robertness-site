Bio

Rob Ness is a strategic account manager and business-intelligence leader with 20 years in corporate travel management. Most recently he was Director of Client Management and Business Intelligence at Sportscorp Travel, where he combined client strategy with travel reporting and analytics for corporate and professional-sports clients. He now runs a small managed-travel consultancy. He lives in the greater Toronto area.

Projects

Self-directed prototypes, built with AI-assisted coding and hosted as static pages on GitHub Pages. The source for each is public.

Lawn Seeding Tracker

A lawn-seeding monitor. It shows hyper-local soil temperature at three depths, daily air highs and lows, and a trend with a seven-day forecast, and gives a plain go or no-go signal for seeding. The aim is guidance that fits a specific lawn rather than a regional average, and ultimately a customized experience for each user. It uses live data from various weather services and runs in the browser with no backend.

StripMeister Copper Recovery Signal

A prototype website widget that turns current copper-market conditions into a plain-language buying signal for recyclers, electricians and contractors weighing a wire stripping tool. Market data refreshes on weekday mornings through a scheduled GitHub workflow. It is a common-sense indicator, not a forecast or financial advice.

Open the live site Source on GitHub

Stock Trading Calculator

A browser-based calculator for U.S. and Canadian securities. It works out how many units a given amount of cash buys, or what a sale returns, after the broker fee, tax on the fee and currency conversion, and shows the break-even price and the gain or loss at a chosen target. Prices are labelled as live, estimated or not live. It is a personal tool, not financial advice.
